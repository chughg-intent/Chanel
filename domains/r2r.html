Strategic Recommendation

Strategic Recommendation

Recommended implementation path with rationale, priority sequence and 90-day quick wins.

Platform-First
Recommended implementation path
Rationale
Across the four Chanel R2R SOPs the portfolio delivers ~$15.7M in estimated annual savings from 35 GO use cases, and the top three clusters — Reconciliation & Verification (8 GOs), Monitoring & Alerting (5) and Decision Intelligence (4) — account for 17 of 35 GOs (~49%), just under the 50% Platform-First threshold, while six canonical agents recur across 2-4 SOPs. A platform-first model is nonetheless recommended because the highest-value pattern (reconciliation/matching) and the foundational data-integration and monitoring services are inherently shared, and consolidation trims implementation cost from ~$2.58M to ~$1.81M (30%). Building shared platform services once — reconciliation/matching, data integration, monitoring, rules/validation, advisory analyzer and governed copilot — avoids four parallel builds and standardizes the human-in-the-loop controls CHANEL's Responsible AI policy requires.

Quick Wins — First 90 Days

  • 2.Stand up the Costing interface health monitor and accrual/reconciling-item aging monitors (payback under 2 months) on Fabric to catch failures and aged items before close
  • 3.Pilot the Journal pre-submission validation agent (payback under 1 month) to lift first-pass approval toward 90%
  • 4.Launch the master-data cleanse (trading-partner codes, common transaction IDs, SKU/location mapping) that unblocks the shared reconciliation service

Long-term Vision
A consolidated set of shared R2R platform services — one reconciliation/matching engine, one data-integration gateway, one monitoring/escalation service, one advisory-analyzer/copilot framework — powers six canonical agents across all four SOPs, with humans retaining every posting, approval and accounting-judgment decision, while low-value long-tail automations (recurring journals, FX revaluation) run as shared RPA.
