Platform-First
Recommended implementation path
Rationale
The portfolio delivers $12.9M in total annual savings across 40 GO use cases with a ~2.4-month blended current payback improving to ~1.7 months consolidated. The top three clusters — Reconciliation & Verification (8 GOs), Exception Management (6 GOs) and Decision Intelligence (5 GOs) — together contain 19 of 40 GOs (48%), just under the 50% Platform-First trigger, but combined with Document Processing and Reporting the shared-service pattern dominates and every SOP consumes the reconciliation service, so a platform-first build maximizes reuse. Consolidating implementation from $2.60M per-SOP to $1.82M shared (30% efficiency) yields $36.9M net three-year benefit and frees ~106 FTE.
