Rationale
The 27 GO use cases across the four S2P SOPs deliver $2,951,828 in annual savings, and the top three clusters - Reconciliation & Verification (C09), Compliance & Regulatory (C05) and Knowledge Synthesis (C10) - together account for 18 of the 27 GO use cases (67%), well above the 50% Platform-First threshold. Because the same five agent patterns (status/inquiry copilot, matching/reconciliation, compliance screening, exception routing, document ingestion) recur across three or four SOPs each, building eight shared platform services once cuts implementation cost from $1,546,000 to $1,082,200 (30%) and blended payback from 6.3 to 4.4 months. A unified S2P agent platform under Chanel TECH governance is therefore clearly preferable to four independent SOP builds.
