Use Case
Automated 100% policy screening engine that checks every invoice and payment request against all required policy rules (supplier eligibility, approval authority, PO compliance, contract compliance, payment terms, tax, sanctions, duplicate risk, bank validation, fraud indicators, spend category, delegation of authority, expense/travel policy) before payment is allowed. Trigger: Invoice approved and queued for payment. Outcome: Zero payments released without complete policy screening; non-compliant items auto-blocked.
Justification
With an automation score of 88/100, annual savings of $3,326,400, and a payback period of only 3.8 months against a $1,060,000 implementation cost, this use case delivers exceptional ROI and directly eliminates compliance risk by ensuring zero payments are released without complete policy screening. Despite high complexity (8/10), the rule-based nature of all 16+ screening dimensions makes full automation both technically viable and strategically imperative.
Top Risks
  • Integration complexity across 10+ source systems (ERP, sanctions databases, tax engines, bank validation APIs, contract management, delegation-of-authority matrices) creates a large failure surface; any single integration outage could block all payment processing.
  • Rule codification errors or incomplete policy translation: with 16+ screening dimensions varying by geography, spend category, PO vs. non-PO, and expense/travel applicability, incorrect rule encoding could either block legitimate payments (false positives causing operational disruption) or pass non-compliant ones (false negatives defeating the purpose).
  • Operational bottleneck from excessive false-positive blocking: if screening rules are overly conservative, a high exception rate could overwhelm the residual manual review capacity and delay payments, damaging supplier relationships and triggering late-payment penalties.
  • Sanctions and fraud indicator data staleness: if sanctions lists, PEP databases, or fraud-pattern models are not refreshed in near-real-time, the engine may clear payments that should be blocked.
  • Change management resistance: AP staff accustomed to manual screening may distrust automated decisions, leading to shadow processes, workarounds, or redundant manual checks that erode the projected 38-FTE efficiency gain.
Key Requirements
  • SAP S/4HANA (or primary ERP) workflow engine and business rules framework
  • SAP Business Rules Management / Decision Tables
  • Sanctions & restricted-party screening tool (e.g., SAP Watch List Screening, Dow Jones, World-Check)
  • Tax compliance engine (e.g., Vertex, Avalara, SAP Tax Compliance)
  • Duplicate invoice detection module (ERP-native or Oversight/AppZen)
  • Bank account validation service (e.g., SWIFT Ref, Accuity/Bankers Almanac, IBAN validation API)
  • Invoice management / AP automation platform (e.g., SAP Ariba, Coupa, OpenText VIM)
  • Fraud indicator scoring rules (configurable rule sets within ERP or middleware)
  • SQL / HANA for real-time data queries and audit logging
  • Workflow orchestration / BPM (SAP Workflow, Signavio, or equivalent)
