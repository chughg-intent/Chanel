Platform-First
Recommended implementation path
Rationale
The portfolio delivers ~$1,014,842 in annual savings across 18 GO use cases with zero NO-GOs, and the top three clusters (Knowledge Synthesis C10, Monitoring C06, Exception Management C08) together with Decision Intelligence C03 account for 11 of 18 GO use cases (>60%), all built on the same Fabric + CRAFT + Biarritz + ServiceNow stack. Consolidating to seven shared platform services cuts implementation cost from $1,015,000 to $710,500 (30%) and blended payback from 12.0 to 8.4 months. Because the same commentary, reconciliation, detection and root-cause agents recur across all four SOPs, a platform-first build is decisively cheaper than four bespoke solutions.

C04 Data Entry Automation (shared data-quality gate)
C06 Monitoring & Alerting (anomaly + model detection)
C08 Exception Management (root-cause & quality review)
C03 Decision Intelligence (baseline forecasting + driver classification)

  • 3.Stand up the numerical + quality validators alongside the commentary pilot so AI drafts are trustworthy from day one (Owner: FP&A Manager)
